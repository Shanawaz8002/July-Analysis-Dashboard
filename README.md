# Little-Twins-Sales-Analysis (Interactive Dashboard & SQL Queries)

## Project Objective
The Little Twins retail store wants to analyze their July sales data. So that, the store manager and management can understand customer buying behavior, peak hours, and product performance to increase revenue.

## Dataset used
- [Little Twins July Sales Excel Dataset]
- <a href= "https://github.com/Shanawaz8002/July-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/July%20Sales%20Dashboard.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs & Metrics)
- What is the Total Revenue (Net Sales)
- What is the Average Order Value (AOV)
- What is the total quantity of products sold.
- What is the total number of transactions.
- What is the average number of items per transaction.
- Daily and Hourly trend of total transactions.
- Percentage of sales by product category and payment modes.
- Top 5 best sellers and bottom 5 worst sellers by quantity sold.

## Process
- Verified data for any missing values, anomalies, and consistency.
- Ensured data types, formats, and values were clean and standardized using SQL Server
- Extracted key performance indicators (KPIs) and trends using SQL queries
- Built pivot tables and designed an interactive dashboard in MS Excel

## Project Insight
- **Top Categories:** Bags, Toys, and Footwear are among the top revenue-contributing product categories.
- **Payment Preferences:** Online and Debit Cards are heavily preferred by customers compared to cash.
- **Peak Hours:** Transactions peak significantly during evening hours (around 7:00 PM) and morning opening hours.
- **Best Sellers:** Bibs, Baby Rompers, and Baby Caps are the highest quantity-selling items.

## Final Conclusion:
To improve sales further at Little Twins, targeted promotions and inventory stocking should focus on top-performing categories like Toys and Bags, while underperforming items (like Baby Socks and Rattles) can be bundled with discounts to clear slow-moving stock.
